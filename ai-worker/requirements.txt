# Blackwell GB10, CUDA 13) the prebuilt CT2 wheel may need to be replaced

# Pin ctranslate2 to the CUDA 12 line on x86_64 (the L4 host). Newer CT2
# wheels are built against CUDA 13 and pull the whole nvidia-*-cu13 stack
# into the venv NEXT TO torch's cu12 wheels — torch then resolves mixed
# libraries and cuDNN dies with CUDNN_STATUS_NOT_INITIALIZED for every
# torch stage (diarization/PANNs/OCR). On the Spark (aarch64, CUDA 13) the
# newer wheel is the correct one, hence the platform marker.
ctranslate2<4.6; platform_machine == "x86_64"
